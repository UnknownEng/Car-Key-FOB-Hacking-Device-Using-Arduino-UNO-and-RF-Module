Notes from the live run:
- The sketch COMPILES and RUNS in Wokwi's cloud builder once the IRremote and
  RF24 libraries are added (Library Manager / libraries.txt). A libraries.txt
  alone is required — Wokwi will not auto-find the headers without it.
- radio.begin() returns false in the simulator (no nRF24 silicon is modelled),
  so the sketch correctly takes its graceful "RF disabled" path instead of
  hanging — confirming the defensive check works.
- The capture->replay logic, hex payload handling, and the rolling-code
  limitation message all execute exactly as designed.
- NOT verified here: real 433 MHz TX/RX, which needs a physical nRF24 module.
